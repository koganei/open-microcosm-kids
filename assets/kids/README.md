# Kids habitat illustrations

`pond.png`, `puddle.png` and `moss.png` were generated specifically for this fork
with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-23. They are decorative
habitat illustrations, not photographs or scientific evidence. All microscope
views and organism thumbnails in the application come from the actual renderer.

These assets are distributed under the repository's GPL-3.0-or-later terms.
No additional font was added; the application uses the existing Apache-2.0
Roboto font and its existing licence in `assets/fonts/`.

## Prompt briefs

- Pond: standalone landscape watercolor and colored-pencil natural-history
  illustration of a quiet freshwater pond with lily pads, reeds, yellow iris,
  mossy stones, distant trees and a dragonfly. Warm ivory paper, sage/forest
  greens, subdued turquoise and golden light. Natural proportions, no text,
  people, interface, framing or cartoon faces.
- Puddle: the same naturalist illustration style; a shallow rain puddle on a
  woodland path, wet earth, grasses, pebbles and fallen oak leaves, reflecting
  the sky and trees. No text, UI, people, frames or enlarged microbes.
- Moss: the same style; a cushion of moss on an old log beside a woodland
  stream, tiny wet leaves and droplets, fern fronds and stones. No text, UI,
  people, frames, fantasy creatures or microbes drawn at visible scale.

The [full prompts](prompts.md) are preserved for reproducibility.
